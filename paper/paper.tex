\begin{abstract}
Legible motion is optimised, not decided. Existing methods report what a local
search found, which cannot establish that no better trajectory exists. This
paper presents an instrument that decides the question for a bounded planar
problem: given a world of convex polygonal obstacles, a finite goal set, a
Boltzmann-rational observer and a budget on path cost, it returns a trajectory
achieving a stated legibility and a bound no trajectory within the budget can
exceed. The gap between them is reported rather than hidden. The bound extends
to trajectories constrained to avoid keep-out zones, which yields a certified
lower bound on what such a constraint costs in legibility.
\end{abstract}
